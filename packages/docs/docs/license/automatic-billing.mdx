By default, a [Remotion for Automators](https://remotion.dev/pricing) plan is pre-paid. You define the amount of renders you want beforehand, and if the number is higher afterwards, you need to manually adjust your license.

By enabling automatic billing, you connect the billing to your [usage](/docs/telemetry).  
Your app reports the amount of renders and you pay the exact amount afterwards.

## Why automatic billing

For you, you don't need to manually update your render counts anymore on a schedule, and you only pay the exact amount of renders you did perform. Without automatic billing, you might buy more renders than you actually need.

For us, this establishes accountability for the actual renders being done and less effort sending out reminders to update render counts.

## Availability of automatic billing

Automatic billing is currently tested with a couple of selected customers before we widely roll it out.  
Contact us at hi@remotion.dev if you would like to opt-in.

## Eligibility for opting in

It is only possible to opt-in if:

- you have enabled the "Remotion for Automators" plan
- you are not grandfathered plan, custom pricing agreement or have a discount applied
- you have at least 1 [submitted telemetry event](/docs/telemetry) over the past 30 days
- your subscription is active and not pending cancellation

## How to opt-in

If automatic billing is enabled for you and you are eligible, go to [remotion.dev/dashboard](https://remotion.dev/dashboard) to opt-in.

Because your license moves from pre-paid to post-paid, you will receive a partial refund for what you pre-paid. For example, if your last payment was $200, and you are half into your billing cycle, we'll refund $100.

## Pricing

With automatic billing, the pricing is the same: $0.01 per render, and a $100/mo minimum.

The difference is that with manual billing, you can only increase your license in amounts of 1000 renders or $10/mo.  
With automatic billing, the charge is exact to the cent.

## Seeing your usage

Once you have opted in, you can see your usage so far on [remotion.dev/dashboard](https://remotion.dev/dashboard) and after a couple of datapoints have been ingested, an estimation of your bill at the end of the cycle.

## Switching back to pre-paid

Is not possible to do yourself. Email hi@remotion.dev if you need this.

## Preventing unexpected spikes in billing

If a bill is more than twice than the previous one, we will review the data first before charging you to prevent unexpected spikes in billing.

If data looks like legitimate growth, we will proceed with the charge, if something seems off, we will contact you before charging.

## Combining with "Remotion for Creators"

If you also hold [Remotion for Creators](https://remotion.dev/pricing) seats, they will also become post-paid.  
You can change the number of seats at any time.

Under this model, there is no proration, however many seats you have at the end of the billing cycle you will be charged.  
Seats also count towards the minimum spend of $100/mo that applies if you have Remotion for Automators enabled.

## If you didn't cover your renders before

If you previously paid less than you had to, you can still enable automatic billing.

If we never contacted you that your license is not in good standing, there are no penalties or retroactive charges.  
However, if we see that you are not covering your renders and are not on automatic billing, we might ask you to compensate for missed renders.

## Cancelling your license

With automatic billing, you can still cancel a Remotion License yourself.  
Go to [remotion.dev/dashboard](https://remotion.dev/dashboard) and follow the cancellation procedure.  
With automatic billing, your license is post-paid, so when you cancel, there will be one final charge.
