## Accuracy

The presets were measured at 9:16 from representative iOS captures of full-green videos in TikTok and Instagram Reels. The interface references use the positions and footprints from those captures instead of estimated placements or advertising templates. The guide scales to other composition dimensions so it remains visible in previews of any aspect ratio.

Live interfaces can vary by device, app version, locale, account state, caption length, and placement. Treat the clear region as a capture-calibrated reference, not a guarantee from either platform. The captured geometry is exact at 9:16 and scaled at other aspect ratios. The guide is visible in Remotion Studio and omitted from rendered output.
