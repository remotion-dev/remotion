In 2026, the way we work has changed, and so has Remotion!

Remotion now has [Agent Skills](/docs/ai/skills) and it has become the default way people make videos with Remotion.

And because we can do more and do it more quickly, we made the [Remotion Studio](/docs/studio) fully interactive and allow you to select, drag, trim, crop, edit stuff.

We made a video about the state-of-the-art way you can make videos in Remotion and how you can iterate fast on your video. Give it a watch!
